Low-Latency Diffusion-Based Audio Synthesizer

Riccardo Simionato
University of Oslo
Paper Code

Abstract

This paper proposes a novel autoregressive and purely convolutional diffusion-based polyphonic synthesizer. The designed model uses a U-net architecture and produces the next spectrogram frames conditioned on symbolic MIDI information. We show that the model can take and produce small window frames, allowing low-latency interaction fundamental for music purposes. The purely convolutional design allows for variable input lengths. The impact of different frame sizes is investigated, and objective metrics and listening tests highlight that 512 as the input length leads to the best results. The model also runs in real-time on the GPU.
